# HR Data Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee data to understand employee attrition and identify factors that may influence employees to leave an organization.

The goal is to explore HR data, identify patterns, create meaningful visualizations, and provide data-driven business recommendations to improve employee retention.

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Compare salaries of employees who stayed and left.
- Understand attrition across different departments.
- Explore age distribution and employee satisfaction.
- Identify potential factors associated with employee turnover.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- VS Code

## Dataset

The project uses an HR Employee Attrition dataset containing employee information such as:

- Age
- Department
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Attrition
- Employee details

## Project Workflow

1. Data Loading
2. Data Cleaning and Preparation
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Employee Attrition Analysis
5. Salary Analysis
6. Data Visualization
7. Business Insights and Recommendations

## Key Findings

- Employees who stayed had an average monthly income of ₹6,832.74.
- Employees who left had an average monthly income of ₹4,787.09.
- The analysis explores differences in attrition across age groups and departments.
- Job satisfaction is examined across departments to identify potential workforce trends.

## Visualizations

The project includes the following charts:

1. Employee Attrition Analysis – Count Plot
2. Attrition by Department – Bar Chart
3. Average Monthly Income Comparison – Bar Chart
4. Employee Age Distribution – Box Plot
5. Employee Attrition by Age – Line Chart
6. Department-wise Attrition – Pie Chart
7. Department vs Job Satisfaction – Heatmap
8. HR Analytics Dashboard

All generated charts are saved in the `output` folder.

## Business Recommendations

- Review compensation structures to improve employee retention.
- Identify departments experiencing higher employee turnover.
- Conduct regular employee satisfaction surveys.
- Provide career development and promotion opportunities.
- Improve employee engagement and work-life balance.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis can help organizations understand employee attrition patterns and explore workforce trends.

The analysis provides useful insights into salary differences, age distribution, departmental attrition, and job satisfaction.

These findings can support better HR decision-making and employee retention strategies.
